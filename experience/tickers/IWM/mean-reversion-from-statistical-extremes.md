# IWM Mean Reversion from Statistical Extremes

**Thesis:** IWM (small-caps) mean-reversion calls are high-probability entries when reaching statistical extremes (RSI/Bollinger %B deep oversold), but are invalidated instantly if the 200-day SMA is broken on volume or fails to hold as a floor.

## Analysis

Small-caps (IWM) are highly sensitive to liquidity and macro-regime shifts. While they frequently bounce from deep oversold RSI (<30), technical integrity of the 200-day SMA is the primary filter for thesis validity.

**The "Regime Pivot" Rule:**
1. **Mean Reversion is valid** as long as IWM stays above its 200-day SMA.
2. **Mean Reversion fails** once the 200-day SMA is broken. Structural damage from macro headwinds (Yields/Energy) overrides technical oversold signals.
3. **Reclamation Failure:** A "relief rally" that reclaims the 200-day SMA but fails to hold it over 24-48 hours is a high-confidence reversal signal indicating a return to a structural bearish regime.

The "Iran Pause" relief rally (March 23) successfully reclaimed the 235 strike and 200 SMA, but the failure to hold these levels on March 24 (breaking down to 232.51) confirms that technical gravity is dominating the news catalyst.
