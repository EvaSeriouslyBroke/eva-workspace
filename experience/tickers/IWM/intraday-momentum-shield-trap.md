# [IWM] Intraday Momentum Shield vs Technical Gravity

**Thesis:** Intraday relative strength ("Momentum Shield") in IWM (small-caps) that suggests a bullish divergence against a weak broader market (SPY/QQQ) is a high-risk trap when the ticker is trading below its 50-day and 200-day SMAs.
**Applies to:** IWM, general
**Tags:** momentum-shield, relative-strength, below-sma, bull-trap, bear, dte-short
**Confidence:** medium
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-25

## Analysis

This experience refines the "Momentum Shield" concept—where a ticker shows relative strength by rising or gapping up while the broader market sells off. 

**The SMA Ceiling:**
While a Momentum Shield is a powerful long signal in a bull regime (above SMAs), it functions as a "Bull Trap" in a bear regime (below SMAs). On 2026-03-25, IWM gapped up and showed early relative strength, but this "shield" shattered as soon as the stock encountered the structural resistance of its 50 and 200 SMAs.

**Key Rule:**
Do not trust intraday relative strength or "Momentum Shields" for long entries if the ticker is below both major SMAs. The structural downtrend ("Technical Gravity") will eventually override intraday momentum, often leading to rapid reversals and "Reclamation Failures."

## Exceptions & Nuances

- **Volume Confirmation:** A Momentum Shield might hold if the gap-up is supported by 2x-3x average relative volume AND the broader market starts to catch up (positive correlation return).
- **Gamma Sensitivity:** Weeklies in this setup are extremely binary; the delta-heavy $225C lost value at an accelerated rate compared to the underlying's move once the floor broke.
