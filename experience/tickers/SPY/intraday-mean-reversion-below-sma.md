# SPY Intraday Mean Reversion Below SMAs

**Thesis:** Tactical "bounce" calls in a structural bear regime (below 50/200 SMAs) based on oversold conditions are low-probability setups that frequently fail as "technical gravity" and EOD volatility override relief rallies.
**Applies to:** SPY
**Tags:** mean-reversion, bull-trap, below-sma, bear, dte-short

## Analysis

When SPY is trading below its 50-day and 200-day SMAs, the technical regime is structurally bearish. Intraday "oversold" signals (RSI recovering from <30-40) often trigger tactical long entries ("mean reversion"), but these bounces are typically weak and vulnerable to rejection at overhead resistance or simply rolling over due to EOD mandate constraints. News catalysts (like "bottom calls") provide temporary support but rarely possess the strength to reclaim major structural floors in a single session.
