# Relief Reclaim Momentum

**Thesis:** Structural uptrends in financial sectors (BAC) favor news-driven "relief" reclaims of the 50/200 SMA cluster when broader market sentiment shifts to "risk-on."
**Applies to:** BAC
**Tags:** bull, dte-short, news-driven, sma-reclaim, relief-rally
**Confidence:** low
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-09

## Analysis

When BAC is in a structural uptrend (above 50 and 200 SMAs), news-driven catalysts (e.g., geopolitical ceasefires) that remove tail risks trigger rapid "re-entry" momentum. The 50/200 SMA cluster serves as a magnetic pivot; once reclaimed, historical relative strength and positive sector rotation provide a tailwind toward resistance (Bollinger Upper Band).
