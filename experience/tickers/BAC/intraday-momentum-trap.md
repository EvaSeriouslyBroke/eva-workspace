# Intraday Momentum Trap

**Thesis:** Intraday Relative Strength (IRS) and reclaiming VWAP are unreliable long signals ("bull traps") for BAC when the stock is trading below its 50 and 200-day SMAs. Technical gravity and broader market (SPY) breakdown will almost always override local momentum.

**Applies to:** BAC
**Tags:** relative-strength, bull-trap, below-sma, momentum, bear, dte-short
**Confidence:** low
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-07

## Analysis

This pattern suggests that BAC's "strength" relative to a weak SPY is often just a lag in participation rather than a true recovery. When the ticker is in a structural bear regime (below 50/200 SMAs), momentum buyers provide the liquidity for institutional sellers to exit into rallies. 

Intraday recovery setups (higher lows, VWAP reclaim) fail to attract follow-through because the higher-timeframe technical pressure (daily/weekly) remains net-negative.

## Exceptions & Nuances

- **The SPY Anchor:** If SPY is also below its own major SMAs, the probability of any single ticker "decoupling" to the upside is extremely low.
- **News Intensity:** Extreme fundamental catalysts (e.g., M&A, massive earnings beat) may temporarily override technicals, but typical "Zacks Top Stock" or partnership news is insufficient.
