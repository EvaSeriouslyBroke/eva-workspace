## Analysis

While secular bear regimes (price below 50/200 SMAs) generally exert downward "Technical Gravity," the sudden removal of a primary macro headwind (binary risk) can trigger a violent re-pricing. In these scenarios, the "gap-and-go" momentum can override traditional resistance levels.

Key success markers for this setup:
1. **Binary Risk Removal:** A specific, identifiable news catalyst (ceasefire, policy reversal) that clears a major market fear.
2. **Benchmark Alignment:** The rally must be broad-based, with SPY showing similar strength and positive trajectory.
3. **SMA Reclamation:** The price must not just hit, but decisively trade through/above the previously lost 50 or 200 SMA levels during the initial session.

Unlike tactical "scalps" in a bear regime, a successful short-squeeze with benchmark alignment can represent a "regime pivot" attempt, though strictly adhering to day-trading rules (EOD close) remains mandatory.
