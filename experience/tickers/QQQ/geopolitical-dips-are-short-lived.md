**Thesis:** QQQ tends to recover quickly (within 3–7 trading days) from geopolitical-driven dips, but mean reversion call plays are only viable if the stock remains positioned above its 200-day SMA; breaks below the 200 SMA invalidate this recovery pattern.

## Analysis

The QQQ "geopolitical fear fade" pattern relies on the large-cap tech sector's balance sheet resilience. When price action is driven by macro headlines rather than tech fundamentals, a mean reversion recovery is a high-probability trade.

Recent 2026 price action establishes the **200 SMA as a high-conviction reclamation signal**. Reclaiming the 50/200 SMA cluster (601/597) on removal of binary risk (e.g. ceasefire news + CPI relief) provides a stable structural "floor" for intraday momentum.

**Key Hindsight Insight (April 10, 2026):**
The successful reclaim and hold of the 50/200 SMA cluster confirms that technical floors override news-driven volatility. Even with a "flat" P&L result on one position due to EOD mandate, the underlying technical structure supported a steady intraday uptrend, validating the "Dip Recovery" thesis when supported by both technical reclaims and macro economic relief.
