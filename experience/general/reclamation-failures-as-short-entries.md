# Reclamation Failures Below SMAs are High-Confidence Short Entries

**Thesis:** A technical attempt to reclaim a major SMA (50 or 200) that fails within 48 hours is a high-confidence signal that a structural bearish regime is continuing, making short entries (puts) highly profitable intraday setups.

**Applies to:** general (multi-ticker pattern)
**Tags:** reclamation-failure, below-sma, bear, dte-short
**Confidence:** medium
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-24

## Analysis

In a structural bearish regime (price below both 50 and 200 SMAs), the 200 SMA acts as a "magnetic floor" that buyers attempt to defend. When this defense fails — meaning the price breaks below, rallies slightly to touch the SMA from below, and then rejects it — it signals the final exhaustion of the dip-buyers. 

**Intraday Timing:** These failures often happen in the first 90 minutes of trading (the "Morning Fade"). If a stock opens near a recently lost SMA, fails to hold a rally above it, and makes a lower-low relative to the morning open, the probability of a trend continuation to the downside is extremely high. 

**Gamma Sensitivity:** Because these moves are often violent as trailing stops are triggered, they are ideal for **short-DTE weekly options** (day trades).

## Exceptions & Nuances

- **Geopolitical "Noise":** If the rejection is purely news-driven (e.g., Iranian peace talk denials), it may reverse just as quickly if the headline changes. Technical confirmation (lower-lows) is required to differentiate "noise" from "regime continuation." 
- **Time Stop Risk:** Day trades on this pattern are highly sensitive to the 3:45 PM exit rule. If the move happens late in the day, the profit window may be too short to overcome the bid/ask spread and theta.
